Phase 1 — The Foundation
Weeks 1–8
Cluster training on the big lifts. 4 days a week: 2 glute-focused days, 1 quad-and-glute day, 1 upper-body and core day. Groove your hip thrust and squat, wake up the glutes, build the strength base everything else grows from.
Strength + SizeWeeks 1–84 days/week
🔥
Phase 2 — The Build
Weeks 9–12
A glute specialization block. 6 days a week hitting the glutes, hamstrings and quads from every angle with hip thrusts, abduction, kickbacks and pull-throughs. Run this after Phase 1 to add real size.
High VolumeWeeks 9–126 days/week
🏆
Phase 3 — The Peak
Weeks 13–16
A peak cycle that alternates heavy strength days and high-volume glute days. Your hip thrust and squat numbers hit their highest here. Everything you built gets put to use.
Peak PerformanceWeeks 13–16Periodized
